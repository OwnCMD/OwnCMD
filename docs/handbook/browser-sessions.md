# Saved browser sign-ins

Reuse website sessions and clear them when necessary.

Applies to: Both

## How saved sessions work

Settings describes website sessions as private to your account and retained for up to 30 days. Websites can expire or revoke sessions sooner, so a saved session does not guarantee you will remain logged in.


## Clear website sessions

Open Settings and the browser section, then select Clear saved sign-ins. Confirm the action. Clearing signs you out of saved websites and closes active OwnCMD browser sessions.


## OwnCMD account versus website account

Clearing saved website sign-ins does not delete your OwnCMD account. If a website requests authentication again, take browser control and sign in there.


## Check whether a session is actually reusable

Open the relevant website in a Work browser and inspect its signed-in state. A remembered cookie is only one part of a website’s authentication policy. The website may require a fresh password, a second factor, or a different account selection. Follow the website’s prompts under human control rather than assuming that a saved session is still valid.

The interface describes saved sessions as account-isolated. This concerns separation between OwnCMD users; it does not convert the remote browser into a private browsing session on your device. The third-party website still receives normal browser requests and applies its own account and session policies.


## Clear sign-ins step by step

Go to Settings and open the browser section. Read Saved browser sign-ins, then select Clear saved sign-ins. The confirmation explains that saved website sign-ins will be removed and active cloud browser sessions closed. Confirm only when you are ready for that interruption.

After clearing, reopen a browser when needed and sign in again to the required website. If you were midway through a form, check whether the website retained the draft; OwnCMD does not promise that a third-party form survives its browser session ending. This action does not change your OwnCMD password or remove your OwnCMD account.


## Use the right account-security control

Clear saved sign-ins is appropriate when you want OwnCMD’s browser to stop reusing website sessions. To revoke access at the website itself, use that website’s account-security controls as well. Clearing local session data is not a substitute for a website-side sign-out-all-devices or credential change when those actions are needed.

If the wrong website account appears, take control, inspect the account, and sign out or clear the saved sessions before continuing. Do not let the agent proceed with a consequential task in an account you have not verified. The stated maximum retention of 30 days is not a guaranteed minimum: website sessions may expire much sooner.

When reporting a login problem, include the website, whether the session was cleared, and the visible error without sharing passwords or one-time codes. This helps distinguish an expired website session from an OwnCMD application-login problem.
